# 0001-Initial-Design-step-workflow

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The step-workflow skill is being created through the interactive interview process

## Decision
Use the interactive interview approach to design and create the skill

## Consequences
### Positive
- Design centered on real user needs
- Reduced risk of building in the wrong direction
- Better understanding of the problem before building

### Negative
- Requires more upfront time in discovery
- Depends on user availability and clarity during the interview
