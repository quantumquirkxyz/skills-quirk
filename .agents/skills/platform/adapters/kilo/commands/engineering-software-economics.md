# software-economics

When to use: Evaluate software decisions through total cost, delay, quality, debt, lock-in, option value, unit economics, and explicit uncertainty without collapsing into generic valuation or FinOps.

## Workflow

1. Frame the decision
2. Build comparable TCO
3. Model delay and options
4. Price quality and debt
5. Stress uncertainty
6. Recommend and hand off

## Deliverables

- SoftwareEconomicsArtifact artifact

## Guardrails

- risk: medium; trust tier: 3; approval required: no
- side effects: none
- isolation: none; model tier: reasoning

Stop when: Alternatives, horizon, cost drivers, demand, delay, quality/debt effects, lock-in and option assumptions are explicit; calculations reconcile; sensitivity exposes decision reversals; the SoftwareEconomicsArtifact validates or is marked blocked with schema errors.
